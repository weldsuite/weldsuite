Add events to your calendar and invite teammates or external guests. {% .lead %}

---

## Create an event

1. Open **WeldCalendar** from the app rail.
2. Click a time slot on the grid, or press **New event**.
3. Enter **title**, **start** and **end** time.
4. Choose which **calendar** to save under (personal or shared).
5. Add **location**, **video link**, or **description** if needed.
6. Invite **guests** by email or by picking workspace members.
7. Save.

Drag events on the grid to reschedule; drag edges to change duration.

---

## Edit or cancel

1. Click the event on the calendar.
2. Update fields in the detail popover or panel.
3. Save changes, or choose **Delete** to remove the event.

Guests receive updates if notifications are enabled.

---

## Reminders

Set **notifications** (email or in-app) before the event starts — for example 10 minutes or 1 day ahead.

---

## Connect Google Calendar

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations** (or WeldCalendar settings).
2. Connect **Google Calendar**.
3. Choose which Google calendars to show in WeldCalendar.

Synced events appear alongside native WeldCalendar events.
