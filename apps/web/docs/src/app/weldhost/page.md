WeldHost is where you register or connect domains, manage DNS records, and prepare your web address for the rest of WeldSuite. {% .lead %}

{% figure src="/images/help/weldhost-domains.png" alt="WeldHost domains screen" caption="Your domains live in WeldHost — the starting point for website, email, and app setup." /%}

---

## What WeldHost does

With WeldHost you can:

- Register a new domain or connect one you already own
- Manage DNS for that domain from inside WeldSuite
- Keep SSL and related settings with the domain
- Point other WeldSuite apps (WeldMail, Help Center, commerce sites) at a WeldHost domain

Once your domain is connected and its nameservers point at WeldHost, you manage records here instead of at your old DNS provider.

---

## Before you manage DNS

The [Add DNS records](/weldhost/manage-dns-records) guide assumes your domain is **already connected** and ready for DNS changes. If the DNS tab is empty or says the zone is not ready yet, finish connecting the domain first — including any ownership verification and nameserver update at your registrar.

Connecting an external domain is covered in [Connect external domain](/weldhost/connect-external-domain).

{% callout title="Not sure if you're ready?" %}
Open your domain in WeldHost and check the **DNS** tab. If you see a list of records (or an empty list you can add to), you're ready to follow the DNS guide.
{% /callout %}
