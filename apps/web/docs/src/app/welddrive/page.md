WeldDrive is file storage for your workspace — upload, organize, share, and restore deleted files. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **My Drive** — your files and folders
- **Shared with me** — files and folders your teammates shared with you
- **Recent** and **Starred** — quick access
- **Uploads** — files uploaded to WeldDrive itself (not the sheets and documents a project keeps here)
- **Trash** — restore or permanently delete; items are deleted for good after 30 days

Open files in preview or linked editors (documents open in the document editor when supported).

---

## Upload and organize

1. Open **WeldDrive**.
2. Click **New** → **File upload**, or drag files into the window.
3. Create **folders** and use **Move to folder** on files and folders to stay organized.
4. **Star** important files for the sidebar shortcut.

Right-click any file or folder for its actions. File and folder names can't contain `/ \ : * ? " < > |`, and two files in one folder can't share a name, whatever the capitals.

---

## Share with teammates

New files and folders are open to everyone in your workspace who has WeldDrive access. You decide who else gets in, and what they can do, per file or folder.

1. Right-click a file or folder → **Share**.
2. Under **Add people**, pick a teammate, choose **Viewer** or **Editor**, and click **Share**.
3. It now shows under **Shared with me** for them.

What the roles can do:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The person who uploaded the file or created the folder. Everything, always. |
| **Editor** | Open, download, rename, move, delete, and share it with others |
| **Viewer** | Open and download it |

To change someone's role or take their access away, open **Share** again and use the menu next to their name. To drop something that was shared with you, pick **Remove me**.

### General access

**General access** in the Share dialog decides who else can open the item:

- **Everyone in the workspace can edit** — the default
- **Everyone in the workspace can view** — everyone can open it, only the owner and editors can change it
- **Restricted** — only the owner and the people added in the dialog

### Folders

Sharing a folder shares everything in it, including subfolders. Making a folder **Restricted** restricts everything in it too, whatever each file's own setting is. The owner of a folder can edit whatever teammates put in it.

{% callout title="Admins" %}
Workspace owners and admins can open every file and folder in WeldDrive, so a former teammate's restricted files never get lost.
{% /callout %}

Files that belong to another module (a project sheet, a CRM attachment) follow that module's access instead, so they can't be shared from WeldDrive. You can still copy their link.
