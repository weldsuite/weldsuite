Forms collect other people's information. These are the rules WeldForms follows and the choices you have. {% .lead %}

---

## Never ask for passwords

Do not use a form to collect passwords, card numbers or other secrets. Every public form carries the footer "Never submit passwords through WeldForms" and a **Report abuse** link. Publishing screens field labels for credential requests and brand impersonation and holds a suspicious form for staff review.

If a form is reported or held, WeldSuite staff can suspend it, or every public form of a workspace. Respondents then see that the form is no longer available. Each staff action is recorded.

## Sensitive fields

Mark a field **sensitive** when it holds personal data such as health, ID or financial details. A sensitive answer is stored and shown to members with permission to read responses, and it is left out of every event, webhook and workflow.

## Partial responses

By default WeldForms remembers only how far someone got, never what they typed, so drop-off numbers work without storing unfinished answers. Turn on **Collect partial responses** in **Settings** if you want the answers of people who did not finish; those are then labelled as unfinished in the table.

## Receipts

A respondent can receive a copy of their answers, **only at a verified address**: one confirmed by a code, or the address an invitation was sent to. A receipt is never sent to an address typed into an anonymous form, which would let anyone email a stranger.

## Files and signatures

Uploaded files and drawn signatures are stored with the response. They are shown to members with permission to read responses and are never included in events.

## Erasing and deleting

You can delete individual responses and erase one person's data across all forms by email address. See [Read the results](/weldforms/results).

## Reporting a form

If you receive a suspicious form, use **Report abuse** at the bottom of the form. Do not enter any information first.
