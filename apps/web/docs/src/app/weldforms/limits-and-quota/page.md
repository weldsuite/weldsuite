WeldForms is included in every plan. What differs is how many **completed responses** each calendar month your plan includes. {% .lead %}

---

{% figure src="/images/help/weldforms-limits.png" alt="WeldForms settings with themes and monthly usage" caption="Your monthly usage sits on the WeldForms settings page." /%}

## What counts

Only completed responses count. Views, partial responses and previews do not.

## Warnings

Workspace admins get an email when you reach 80% and again at 100% of the monthly quota.

## At the limit

Once the quota is used up, your forms show "This form is not accepting responses right now". Anyone already inside a form can still submit, so nobody loses answers they were typing.

To raise the limit, change plan under **Settings** > **Billing**. The quota starts again at the beginning of the next month.

## Limits you set per form

In **Settings** you can also set open and close dates and a maximum number of responses for a single form, and lower the rate limits that protect a public form from bots.

## AI

AI features (generating a form, summarising answers) are charged to your prepaid AI credits, not to the response quota. They are available on Global only.
