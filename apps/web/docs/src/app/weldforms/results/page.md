The **Results** tab has three views. {% .lead %}

---

## Summary

A chart for every question: bars for choices, the NPS split, the average and spread of ratings, statistics for numbers, and a word list for open text. On Global, an **AI summary** groups open answers into themes with counts, example quotes and sentiment, plus a summary of the whole form. Summaries are cached and regenerated on demand, and charged to your AI credits.

{% figure src="/images/help/weldforms-results-summary.png" alt="The Results summary with an AI summary and an NPS chart" caption="A chart for every question." /%}

## Responses

A table with one row per response.

- Pick columns, including those from earlier versions of the form.
- Filter by answer, date, status and score, and search.
- Open a response in the drawer to read it, see what each integration did with it, and retry a failed step.
- **Delete** responses you no longer need.

Unfinished responses appear only when the form collects **partial responses**, labelled as such; one untouched for 24 hours counts as abandoned.

{% figure src="/images/help/weldforms-results-responses.png" alt="The responses table" caption="One row per response, with the columns you pick." /%}

## Insights

Views, starts, completion rate, average time, **drop-off per question**, and a breakdown by day and by source. Drop-off and time work for every form, also when partial answers are not stored.

{% figure src="/images/help/weldforms-results-insights.png" alt="The Insights view with activity by day and drop-off" caption="Views, starts, completions and where people drop off." /%}

---

## Export

Export responses as **CSV** or **XLSX** with the same columns as the table. Files are included as links. You need permission to export responses.

## Erase a person's data

For a data request, erase everything one respondent submitted across all your forms by email address. Deleting a single response is available from the table and the drawer.

{% callout title="Permissions" %}
Seeing a form and its counts is separate from reading answers, exporting them and deleting them. Workspace admins can grant each separately.
{% /callout %}

## Notifications

Anyone subscribed to a form gets a notification for every response, or a daily digest. You are subscribed to forms you create.
