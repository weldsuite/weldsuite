WeldForms builds forms, surveys and quizzes that people enjoy filling in, and sends the answers on to WeldCRM, WeldDesk, WeldFlow and WeldSheets. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Build** a form from a blank page, a template, or a description you give the AI (Global only).
- **Ask** with text, choice, scale, file upload, signature, contact and address questions.
- **Branch** with logic, variables and quizzes that end on a score.
- **Share** by link, QR code, embed on a website, or personal email invitations with reminders.
- **Decide who answers**: anyone with the link, people with a verified email, or only workspace members.
- **Read** the answers as a summary, a table of responses and insights on where people drop off.
- **Connect** each response to a CRM person, a support ticket, a task, a sheet or a WeldConnect workflow.

## Two layouts

Every form has one layout, chosen in the builder:

- **Conversational** (default): one question at a time, keyboard first, the way most people expect a modern survey to feel.
- **Classic**: all questions on a page, with optional page breaks.

Both run the same logic, so you can switch while you build and check the result in the live preview on desktop or phone.
