## Open the App Store

1. Sign in to WeldSuite.
2. Click **App Store** in the app rail (or go to `/appstore`).
3. Browse available apps — WeldCRM, WeldMail, WeldFlow, WeldHost, and others.

Each card shows what the app does and whether it is already installed in your workspace.

---

## Install an app

1. Click the app you want.
2. Read the description and requirements.
3. Click **Install** (or **Enable**).
4. The app icon appears in the left rail when installation finishes.

Installed apps are shared for the whole workspace. Teammates still need the right [permissions](/settings/team-and-permissions) to use them.

---

## Remove or manage apps

Workspace owners and admins can uninstall apps from the App Store detail page. Removing an app hides it from the rail; data already stored for that app is kept according to your workspace policy.

Per-app settings (mail accounts, desk inboxes, etc.) live under **Settings** once the app is installed.
