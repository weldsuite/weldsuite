---

## What a workspace is

A **workspace** is your organization's home in WeldSuite. Everyone on your team belongs to one workspace (or several, if you work with multiple companies). Inside a workspace you:

- Install the apps your team needs from the **App Store**
- Share contacts, files, and conversations according to permissions
- Manage billing and settings in one place

You switch workspaces from the account menu if you belong to more than one.

---

## How navigation works

WeldSuite uses a consistent layout across apps:

1. **App rail** (far left) — icons for each installed app. Click one to switch modules.
2. **Module sidebar** — navigation specific to the app you are in (for example **Domains** in WeldHost or **Inbox** in WeldMail).
3. **Main content** — lists, detail panels, and editors for the task at hand.
4. **Header** — search, notifications, calendar, and the assistant panel.

Many apps open records in a **side panel** so you can read a contact or domain without leaving the list you were on.
