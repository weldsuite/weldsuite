WeldChat is team messaging — channels for groups, direct messages for one-on-one, and voice/video calls. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Channels** — public or private rooms by team, project, or topic
- **Direct messages** — private chats with one or more people
- **Threads** — reply in context without cluttering the main channel
- **Calls** — start voice or video from a DM or channel
- **Activity** — mentions, reactions, pins, and bookmarks

The sidebar lists channels (often grouped in sections), DMs, and activity feeds.
