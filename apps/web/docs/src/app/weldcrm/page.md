WeldCRM is your workspace CRM — people, companies, deals, tasks, and email sequences in one place. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Contacts** — store people with email, phone, company, and custom fields
- **Companies** — organize accounts with related contacts and activity
- **Deals** — track opportunities through customizable pipelines
- **Tasks** — personal to-dos and follow-ups tied to records
- **Lists** — saved views and segments in the module sidebar
- **Sequences** — automated email drips for outreach

Open any contact or company from a list to see details in a side panel — notes, emails, calls, meetings, and related deals stay on one screen.

## Tips

- Use **filters** and **groups** on list pages to focus on active leads or stale deals.
- Log **notes** on a record after every call so teammates see context.
- Connect WeldMail so email threads appear on contact timelines.
