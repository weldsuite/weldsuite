Create and maintain the people and companies your team sells to and supports. {% .lead %}

---

## Open the contacts area

1. Open **WeldCRM** from the app rail.
2. Use the module sidebar to choose **People** or **Companies** (or a custom list you created).
3. The main table shows names, status, owner, and key fields.

Click a row to open the record in a side panel without losing your place in the list.

---

## Add a person

1. Click **New** (or **Add contact**) on the People list.
2. Enter name, email, phone, and company as needed.
3. Assign an **owner** if your workspace uses ownership.
4. Save.

You can also create a person from inside a company record or import contacts through settings integrations.

---

## Add a company

1. Go to **Companies** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New company**.
3. Fill in name, website, industry, and address fields your workspace uses.
4. Save, then link people from the company's **Contacts** tab.

---

## Log notes and activity

1. Open a contact or company from the list.
2. Use the **Notes** tab (or activity timeline) in the panel.
3. Write what happened on a call or meeting and save.

Notes are visible to teammates with access to that record.

---

## Search and filter

- Use the **search** box on any list for name, email, or company.
- Add **filters** for status, owner, tags, or custom fields.
- Save frequent filters as **lists** in the sidebar for one-click access.
