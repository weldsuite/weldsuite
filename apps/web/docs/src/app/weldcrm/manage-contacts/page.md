Create and maintain the people and companies your team sells to and supports. {% .lead %}

---

## Open the contacts area

1. Open **WeldCRM** from the app rail.
2. Use the module sidebar to choose **People** or **Companies** (or a custom list you created).
3. The main table shows names, status, owner, and key fields.

Click a row to open the record in a side panel without losing your place in the list.

---

## Add a person

1. Click **New person** on the People list.
2. Enter first name, last name, email, job title, and phone as needed.
3. Click **Create person**.
4. Click the new row to open the record in a side panel. Link the person to a company from the **Companies** tab and add more details there.

{% video src="/videos/help/weldcrm-manage-contacts.mp4" poster="/videos/help/weldcrm-manage-contacts.jpg" title="Add a contact in WeldCRM" caption="Create a person from the People list and open the new record." /%}

You can also create a person from inside a company record or import contacts with **Import/Export** on the list.

---

## Add people automatically

WeldCRM can add the people your team works with by itself, so nobody has to click **Add to CRM**. An admin turns it on in **Settings → Apps → WeldCRM → Auto-add**.

- **Email**: people you email, and people who reply to an email you sent. Someone who only writes in is not added until you answer.
- **Meetings**: people outside your workspace you invite to a WeldMeet meeting.
- **Helpdesk**: customers an agent has replied to in WeldDesk.

Colleagues, your own email domains and automated senders such as `noreply@` are never added. Turn on **Also add their company** to link each person to the company of their email domain (jan@acme.com goes to Acme, which is created if it isn't in WeldCRM yet). Free email addresses such as gmail.com never become a company.

Auto-add is available on WeldSuite EU and self-hosted installs.

---

## Add a company

1. Go to **Companies** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New company**.
3. Fill in name, website, industry, and address fields your workspace uses.
4. Save, then link people from the company's **Contacts** tab.

---

## Log notes and activity

1. Open a contact or company from the list.
2. Use the **Notes** tab (or activity timeline) in the panel.
3. Write what happened on a call or meeting and save.

Notes are visible to teammates with access to that record.

---

## Search and filter

- Use the **search** box on any list for name, email, or company.
- Add **filters** for status, owner, tags, or custom fields.
- Save frequent filters as **lists** in the sidebar for one-click access.
