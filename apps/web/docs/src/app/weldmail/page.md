WeldMail brings your team email into WeldSuite — read, reply, and organize without leaving the platform. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- Connect **Gmail**, **Microsoft**, or other accounts per user
- Read a **unified inbox** or switch between connected accounts
- **Compose**, reply, forward, and schedule messages
- Organize with **labels**, stars, archive, snooze, and trash
- See CRM context when WeldCRM is installed (contact side panel)

Folders in the sidebar include Inbox, Starred, Sent, Drafts, Scheduled, Snoozed, Archive, and Trash. Custom **labels** appear below system folders.

| Task | Guide |
