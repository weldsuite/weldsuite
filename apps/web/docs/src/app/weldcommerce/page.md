WeldCommerce runs your online store inside WeldSuite — catalog, checkout, and order fulfillment hooks. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Products** — titles, variants, pricing, images, and inventory links
- **Categories** — organize the catalog for your storefront
- **Orders** — payment status, fulfillment, and customer details
- **Customers** — buyers linked to people/companies when CRM is connected
- **Settings** — branding and customer portal options

Open **Overview** for dashboard counts or jump straight to **Products** or **Orders** in the sidebar.

---

## Typical flow

1. Create **products** and **categories**.
2. Connect payment and shipping integrations in **Settings**.
3. Publish the storefront (domain often managed in [WeldHost](/weldhost)).
4. Process **orders** as they arrive — mark shipped, refund, or contact the buyer.
