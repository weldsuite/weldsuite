WeldDesk is customer support — unified inbox, tickets, knowledge base, and optional live chat widget. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Inbox** — email, chat, and ticket conversations in one queue
- **Tickets** — track status, priority, assignee, and SLA
- **Contacts** — support customers linked to conversation history
- **Knowledge base** — publish help articles for self-service
- **Chat widget** — embed on your site for live conversations
- **Analytics** — volume, response time, and team performance

Sidebar views often include All, Email, Live chat, Tickets, and Archived.
