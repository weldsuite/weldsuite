WeldMeet hosts video meetings inside WeldSuite — instant rooms, scheduled calls, and history. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Start instant meeting** — share a link with guests
- **Schedule** — plan ahead with calendar integration
- **Upcoming** — see meetings starting soon
- **History** — revisit past meetings and participants

Meetings can run in the full-page view or a minimized widget while you work in other apps.

---

## Start a meeting

1. Open **WeldMeet** from the app rail.
2. Click **New meeting** or **Start now**.
3. Allow camera and microphone when prompted.
4. Copy the **invite link** for external guests.

Guests join in the browser without installing desktop software.
