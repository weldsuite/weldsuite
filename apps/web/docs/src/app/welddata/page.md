WeldData helps you find business contacts and companies, then save them to lists for outreach. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Search** people and companies with filters (industry, location, size, etc.)
- **Lists** — saved collections of leads in the sidebar
- **Export to CRM** — push saved leads to [WeldCRM](/weldcrm) as people or companies
- **Exclude saved** — hide leads you already captured
