**Settings** is where workspace owners and admins configure WeldSuite — team, billing, integrations, and per-app options. {% .lead %}

---

## Main areas

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Team** | Members, roles, invites |
| **Permissions** | Fine-grained access per app |
| **Billing** | Plan, payment method, invoices |
| **Integrations** | Google, Shopify, CRM sync, webhooks |
| **Notifications** | Email and in-app preferences |
| **Appearance** | Theme and display options |
| **API keys** | Programmatic access for developers |
| **Per-app settings** | WeldMail accounts, WeldDesk inboxes, phone numbers, etc. |

Open **Settings** from the app rail or your profile menu.

---

## Who can change what

- **Owners** — full access including billing and deletion
- **Admins** — most settings except sensitive billing actions (workspace-dependent)
- **Members** — personal preferences and connected accounts (mail, calendar)

See [Team and permissions](/settings/team-and-permissions) for inviting users and assigning roles.
