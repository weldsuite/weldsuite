You can delete your WeldSuite account yourself. It takes a minute and cannot be undone. {% .lead %}

One WeldSuite account signs you in to every WeldSuite app, including **WeldHR**, WeldMail, WeldChat, WeldFlow, WeldBooks, WeldDesk and WeldCalendar, so deleting it removes your access to all of them.

---

## Delete from the web

1. Sign in at [app.weldsuite.org](https://app.weldsuite.org).
2. Open **Settings → Security**.
3. Under **Danger zone**, choose **Delete account**.
4. Type `DELETE` to confirm and press **Delete account**.

## Delete from a mobile app

In **WeldHR**: tap your workspace at the top of Home to open your **Profile**, then tap **Delete account** at the bottom and confirm. If you have no workspace yet, the **Delete account** link is on the "Create your workspace" screen.

In **WeldFlow** and **WeldCalendar**: **Settings → Privacy → Delete account**.

In the other apps, use the web steps above.

## If you can't sign in

Email [privacy@weldsuite.com](mailto:privacy@weldsuite.com) from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm by email before deleting anything.

---

## What is deleted

- Your WeldSuite login: name, email address, profile picture and sign-in methods.
- Your membership of every workspace.
- Every workspace in which you are the **only** member, including all of its data.
- Your devices' push notification tokens.

## What is kept

- **Data that belongs to a workspace you were part of with others.** That workspace is your employer's or your team's, not yours: messages you sent, tasks, tickets, documents and, in WeldHR, your employee record, leave, payslips and expense claims stay with the workspace. Ask the workspace's administrator (for HR data, your HR department) to delete them.
- **Records we must keep by law**, such as invoices for paid subscriptions, for as long as the law requires.

{% callout title="Are you the only admin of a workspace?" %}
You can't delete your account while you are the only administrator of a workspace that still has other members. Make someone else an administrator in **Settings → Team**, or remove the other members, then delete your account.
{% /callout %}
