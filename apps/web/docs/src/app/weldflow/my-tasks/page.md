See everything assigned to you and update status as work moves forward. {% .lead %}

---

## Open My Tasks

1. Open **WeldFlow** from the app rail.
2. Click **My Tasks** in the module sidebar (or land on `/weldflow` by default).
3. Tasks appear in list or board layout depending on your view setting.

Each row shows title, project, due date, priority, and status.

---

## Update a task

1. Click a task to open the detail panel.
2. Change **Status** (for example To do → In progress → Done).
3. Edit **due date**, **assignee**, or **description** as needed.
4. Add **comments** for teammates watching the task.

Changes save automatically or when you click **Save**, depending on the field.

---

## Filter and sort

- Filter by **project**, **status**, or **due date**.
- Sort by due date or priority to focus on what is urgent.
- Use **search** to find a task by title.

---

## Create a quick task

1. Click **New Task** on My Tasks.
2. Enter a title (and a description if you like).
3. Optionally set the **priority**, **labels**, **due date**, **assignees**, and the **project** the task belongs to.
4. Click **Create task**.

For structured work with milestones, create tasks inside a [project](/weldflow/create-project) instead.

## Mark a task as complete

Tick the checkbox at the start of the row. The task is crossed out and moves to the **Done** group. Untick it to reopen the task.
