WeldSocial plans and publishes content across social accounts — queue, calendar, campaigns, and approvals. {% .lead %}

---

## What you can do

- **Queue** — upcoming posts per connected account
- **Calendar** — month view of scheduled content
- **Drafts** — work in progress before scheduling
- **Campaigns** — group posts for launches
- **Analytics** — engagement metrics per network
- **Approvals** — review step before posts go live

Connect accounts in **Settings** or the module's account manager.

---

## Typical flow

1. Connect **social accounts**.
2. Compose posts with text, links, and media.
3. Schedule or add to **queue**.
4. Run **approvals** if your team requires sign-off.
5. Review **analytics** after publish.
