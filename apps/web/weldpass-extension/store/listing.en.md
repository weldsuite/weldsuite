# Store listing (English)

**Name:** WeldPass

**Summary** (132 characters at most; the same text as `extDescription` in
`src/locales/en.json`, which the manifest uses):

> Fill and save your team's WeldPass logins, right in the fields where you need them.

**Category:** Productivity

**Language:** English (and Dutch, see `listing.nl.md`)

**Description:**

> WeldPass is the password manager of WeldSuite. This extension brings your
> WeldPass logins to the sites where you use them.
>
> - **Fill in place.** A WeldPass icon in login fields opens the logins saved
>   for that site. Pick one and WeldPass fills it in. It also fills login forms
>   inside a frame, and tells you which site that frame belongs to.
> - **Save and update.** After you sign in somewhere, WeldPass offers to save
>   the login, or to update the saved password when you changed it.
> - **Two-step sign-in.** On a one-time-code field, WeldPass offers the current
>   2FA code of the matching login.
> - **New passwords.** On sign-up and change-password forms, WeldPass suggests
>   a strong password and fills it in.
> - **Search.** Find any login from the toolbar button: copy the username,
>   the password or the 2FA code, or open the site.
>
> **Safe by design.** WeldPass fills a login only on `https://` pages whose
> site matches the login, and only after you pick it. The menu with your
> logins is shown in a frame of the extension's own, so the page can't read
> it. Passwords are decrypted by your WeldSuite server at the moment the
> extension needs one (to fill or copy it, or to check whether a password you
> just typed is new), and never stored by the extension. A login you just typed is
> kept in memory, for at most five minutes, until you choose to save it.
>
> **Where your data lives.** The extension signs in to WeldSuite EU
> (`app.weldsuite.eu`) by default, or to your organisation's self-hosted
> WeldSuite. It talks to no other server.
>
> You need a WeldSuite account with access to WeldPass.
