# Mailboxes and domains

Outreach mail goes out from dedicated mailboxes on sending domains of their own, never from your main company domain. Each mailbox warms up before it may join a [campaign](/reach-campaigns). Through the API these are **read-only**: every mailbox is billed at $5 per month, so creating, pausing and deleting mailboxes, and connecting and disconnecting domains, stays in the WeldReach app. All endpoints here require the `reach_mailboxes:read` scope. {{ className: 'lead' }}

<ResourceVersionBanner />

<Note>
  WeldReach is available on request only. Until WeldSuite approves the workspace, every endpoint here returns `403 APP_ACCESS_REQUIRED`. See the [WeldReach overview](/weldreach).
</Note>

## The mailbox model

### Properties

<Properties>
  <Property name="id" type="string">Unique identifier (e.g. `rmbx_abc123`).</Property>
  <Property name="domainId" type="string">The [domain](#the-domain-model) the mailbox is on.</Property>
  <Property name="domainName" type="string">That domain's name.</Property>
  <Property name="email" type="string">The mailbox address.</Property>
  <Property name="firstName" type="string">Sender first name. Fills `senderFirstName` in templates.</Property>
  <Property name="lastName" type="string">Sender last name. Fills `senderLastName` in templates.</Property>
  <Property name="signature" type="string">Plain-text signature added below every email, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="type" type="string">`google` (a Google Workspace inbox) or `shared` (an inbox on shared mail servers).</Property>
  <Property name="status" type="string">See [statuses](#mailbox-statuses).</Property>
  <Property name="warmupDay" type="integer">Days of warmup sending so far.</Property>
  <Property name="warmupStatus" type="string">The mailbox's state in the warmup network: `waiting`, `warming`, `maintaining`, `paused`, `error` or `removed`, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="warmupInboxRate" type="number">Share of this mailbox's warmup mail that reached the inbox (not spam) over the last 3 days, from 0 to 1. `null` until there are at least 10 judged emails.</Property>
  <Property name="warmupSent7d" type="integer">Warmup emails sent in the last 7 days.</Property>
  <Property name="warmupSpamRescues7d" type="integer">Warmup emails to this mailbox that were moved out of spam in the last 7 days.</Property>
  <Property name="warmupBlocker" type="string">Why a warming mailbox may not join campaigns yet, or `null`. See [warmup](#warmup).</Property>
  <Property name="dailyCap" type="integer">Most campaign emails this mailbox sends per day, 1 to 50.</Property>
  <Property name="rampCurrent" type="integer">Today's ramp-up limit. A new sender starts low and goes up every day until it reaches `dailyCap`.</Property>
  <Property name="sentToday" type="integer">Campaign emails sent today.</Property>
  <Property name="lastSentAt" type="timestamp">When the mailbox last sent, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="pausedReason" type="string">Why the mailbox is paused, or `null`. For example `user`, `bounces`, `login_failed`, `low_inbox_rate` or `domain_inactive`.</Property>
  <Property name="lastError" type="string">The last error, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="createdAt" type="timestamp">When the mailbox was created.</Property>
</Properties>

Login credentials are never returned.

### Mailbox statuses {{ id: 'mailbox-statuses' }}

| `status` | Sends campaigns | Meaning |
|----------|-----------------|---------|
| `provisioning` | No | Being set up at the mail provider. |
| `warming` | No | Building its sending reputation. Can already be assigned to a campaign and answer [replies](/reach-threads#reply-to-a-thread). |
| `active` | Yes | Ready. Only active mailboxes send campaign email. |
| `paused` | No | Paused by a person or by WeldReach; see `pausedReason`. |
| `suspended` | No | Suspended; see `pausedReason` and `lastError`. |
| `error` | No | Needs attention; see `lastError`. |
| `deleting`, `deleted` | No | Being removed, or removed. |

### Warmup {{ id: 'warmup' }}

A new mailbox exchanges automatically generated emails with other WeldReach mailboxes to build its reputation. It becomes `active`, and can send campaign email, once all of these hold:

- it has warmed up for at least 14 days;
- at least 85% of its warmup mail reached the inbox over the last 3 days;
- it has no login problem.

While it is warming, `warmupBlocker` says what it is waiting for:

| `warmupBlocker` | Meaning |
|-----------------|---------|
| `too_early` | Fewer than 14 days of warmup so far. |
| `not_enough_data` | Too few warmup emails were judged in the last 3 days to compute an inbox rate. |
| `low_inbox_rate` | Less than 85% of its warmup mail reached the inbox. |
| `login_error` | The warmup network cannot log in to the mailbox. This needs action in the app. |
| `pool_too_small` | The workspace has mailboxes on fewer than 3 domains. Warmup pairs mailboxes across domains, so it needs at least 3. |

## The domain model

A sending domain is a WeldHost domain of the workspace that is connected to WeldReach. WeldReach sets up its mail records.

### Properties

<Properties>
  <Property name="id" type="string">Unique identifier (e.g. `rdom_abc123`).</Property>
  <Property name="hostDomainId" type="string">The WeldHost domain.</Property>
  <Property name="name" type="string">The domain name.</Property>
  <Property name="mailpoolDomainId" type="string">The domain's id at the mail provider, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="status" type="string">`connecting`, `active`, `error` or `disconnecting`.</Property>
  <Property name="dnsStatus" type="object">The last DNS check, or `null`: `spf`, `dkim`, `dmarc` and `mx` (each a boolean) and `checkedAt`.</Property>
  <Property name="dmarcPolicy" type="string">The published DMARC policy (such as `none` or `quarantine`), or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="connectedAt" type="timestamp">When the domain was connected, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="lastError" type="string">The last error, or `null`.</Property>
  <Property name="mailboxCount" type="integer">Mailboxes on this domain.</Property>
  <Property name="createdAt" type="timestamp">When the domain was added.</Property>
</Properties>
