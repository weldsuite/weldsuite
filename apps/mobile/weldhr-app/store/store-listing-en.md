## Full description (both, max 4000 chars)
WeldHR is the mobile app for WeldSuite HR. Employees handle their own HR from their phone, and managers approve their team's requests wherever they are.

FOR EVERY EMPLOYEE
- Clock in and out
- Your schedule: your shifts and hours worked, and open shifts you can sign up for
- Request time off and see your leave balances before you ask
- Report sick and follow your sick leave
- Claim expenses with a photo of the receipt
- Payslips and annual statements, when your employer runs payroll in WeldSuite
- Announcements from HR, onboarding tasks, reviews and coaching notes
- Push notifications when a request is approved or declined

FOR MANAGERS AND HR
- See who is in, on leave or sick today
- Approve or decline leave and expense claims in one tap
- Plan shifts and handle shift requests
- The team directory

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Lock the app with Face ID or your fingerprint
- Documents are downloaded over your own session and never get a public link
- Location is only asked when your employer requires you to be at work to clock in, and only at that moment

Available in English and Dutch, in light and dark mode.

REQUIREMENTS
WeldHR needs a WeldSuite workspace with WeldHR, and an employee record linked to your account. Ask your HR department for access. Everything syncs with the WeldHR module on the web at app.weldsuite.org.
