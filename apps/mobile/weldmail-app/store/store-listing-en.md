## Full description (both, max 4000 chars)
WeldMail is the mobile companion for WeldSuite email. Pull up your inbox, triage unread mail, and send a reply without opening a laptop.

Built for founders, operators, and anyone who lives in email between meetings, WeldMail gives you the fastest path to the thread you need right now.

FEATURES
- Unified inbox across your WeldSuite mailboxes
- Labels and folders with a one-tap drawer
- Unread, starred, and attachment filters
- Message threads with reply, reply all, and forward
- Compose with rich formatting, attachments, and schedule send
- Search across from, to, subject, and labels
- Push notifications for new mail
- Light and dark mode, synced with your system settings
- Secure sign-in with your WeldSuite account (Clerk-powered authentication)
- Enterprise workspace support

REQUIREMENTS
- An active WeldSuite workspace (sign up at weldsuite.com)
- At least one mail account connected in WeldMail

WeldMail is the mobile-first extension of the WeldSuite platform. All changes you make in the app sync with the web version at app.weldsuite.org.
