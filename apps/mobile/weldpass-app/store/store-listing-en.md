## Full description (both, max 4000 chars)
WeldPass is the password manager of WeldSuite. Keep your own logins in your personal vault, share the team's logins in shared vaults, and reach all of them from your phone.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
- Logins, secure notes and payment cards
- Your personal vault, and the shared vaults your team gave you access to
- Search across every vault by title, username or site
- Two-factor codes with a countdown, so you don't need a separate authenticator for shared accounts

STRONG PASSWORDS
- A generator for long, random passwords without look-alike characters
- A strength meter as you type
- Password health: see which passwords are weak, reused or old

SAFE ON YOUR PHONE
- Always locked with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your phone's passcode
- Asks again before it shows or copies a password
- Copied passwords leave the clipboard after 30 seconds
- WeldPass is hidden in the app switcher and blocks screenshots of your items
- Every time a password is shown, it's recorded in the vault's activity

YOUR DATA STAYS IN THE EU
WeldPass runs on WeldSuite EU, or on your organisation's own WeldSuite server. Vaults are encrypted at rest with keys that never leave the server.

Available in English and Dutch, in light and dark mode.

REQUIREMENTS
WeldPass needs a WeldSuite account in a workspace with WeldPass. Everything syncs with WeldPass on the web and in the browser extension.
