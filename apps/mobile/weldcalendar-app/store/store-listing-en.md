## Full description (both, max 4000 chars)
WeldCalendar is the mobile companion for WeldSuite scheduling. Check today's agenda, flip through the month, manage calendars, and create or edit events from your phone.

Built for teams and individuals who live in WeldSuite — whether you use workspace calendars at work or a personal calendar on the same account.

FEATURES
- Agenda view with today's and upcoming events at a glance
- Month grid to scan the whole schedule
- Calendars list for workspace and personal calendars
- Create and edit events with title, time, location, and calendar
- Push reminders when events are coming up
- Light and dark mode, synced with your system settings
- Secure sign-in with your WeldSuite account (Clerk-powered authentication)
- Works with workspace calendars and personal WeldCalendar

REQUIREMENTS
- A WeldSuite account (sign up at weldsuite.org)
- For workspace calendars: membership in a WeldSuite workspace

WeldCalendar syncs with the web experience at weldcalendar.weldsuite.org and the platform calendar module at app.weldsuite.org.
